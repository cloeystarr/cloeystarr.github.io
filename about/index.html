Now

From GTM to building AI products

PipelineOM → ompom.ai

Building on my experience in enterprise GTM, I developed PipelineOM with Chris Starr around a practical problem: turning fragmented data into context that AI tools can use. That work became the foundation for my next product. After we concluded PipelineOM and Chris joined Arena, I carried the IP we had developed into ompom.ai.

Today, I’m the sole founder of ompom.ai, coding the product myself with Codex. I’m bringing years of work with customers, creative tools and communities into building software that helps people own their context and use it to make things. Chris continues to advise.

A little more context

Questions.

What kind of work do you make?

I write poetry and personal essays, make poetry films and field films, and build tools around personal context. You can explore the work through Poetry, Events and Portals.

What is Portals?

Portals is my illustrated studio journal. It brings together films, field notes and experiments in making, including what I tried, what changed and the sources that became part of the work.

Did you work in film before using AI?

Yes. While I was in college, I played the lead in Deconstructing Paisley, directed by Mike Mazzella. The original trailer is included above.

How do you use AI in your creative work?

I use AI to work with my writing, photographs and archive, including experiments in animation and filmmaking. Each project has its own process and credits. I write about those decisions in Portals.

What is ompom.ai?

I’m the sole founder of ompom.ai. I’m building around the idea that people should own their personal context and be able to build themselves on the internet.

How can someone contact you about a collaboration?

You can reach me on LinkedIn to discuss a project. My films, writing and professional background are here to give you a sense of the work.
