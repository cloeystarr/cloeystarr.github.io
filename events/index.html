October 14, 2026More Worlds to Build
Read the Portals story →
Credits & original releases

Notes

Yi Hua of World Labs, Yanpei Cao of VAST/Tripo, and Jimei Yang of Runway showed tools that change what an independent filmmaker can attempt: moving through captured moments, turning reference images into 3D assets, transforming existing footage.

My archive is full of photographs, performances and unfinished films. I want to turn that material into stories I author myself. That’s why I’m building OM/POM.

This field film brings my perspective together with footage from Aiko Dai, Aniket J, and Romy Ilano, plus the Crayon builder showcase.

Speaker portraits: Yi Hua, Yanpei Cao and Jimei Yang, sourced from their websites. Model demonstrations: World Labs, Tripo and Runway.

OM/POM artwork: Chloe Starr and Chris Starr AI. Poem animation: Google Flow, with OpenAI reference artwork. Edited with Codex and FFmpeg.

Music: “Covert Affair” by Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0. Full rolling credits in the film.
