Poem 8 · Chloe Starr

i thought i had died today. i was very angry at my mother and i didn't look all the way before crossing the street. it was the wind of the swerve of the white pick-up truck that shook me back into consciousness. i finished riding across the street and into the triangle. watching my hands shake wondering if i was real anymore. perhaps death doesn't hurt and i had not felt. I felt for my missing brain and wondering if it was still there. If I was still real or solid or a ghost. An old guy on a two-person bicycle asked me for directions to the saucer. Hence, I was still real
