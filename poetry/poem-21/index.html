Poem 20 · Chloe Starr

to my mother I have four kids. you wore it like a badge. I was five helping raise the twins. you crawled into my bed. your father was dead. vodka and crying. I made room. in Texas I woke to my cousin in my boyfriend’s bed. he beat me. locked me in a closet. you knew and posted about her on Facebook. you chose her. when your father died I held you. when mine died you turned everyone against me. he was the only one in that family who loved me. you knew that too. my brothers followed you. I lost my father and somehow I was the thing everyone needed to get away from. a triangle. the evil eye inside. I need a reminder on my skin that you lie. I still want to believe my mother. you lied. said I only wanted money. made me the problem so nobody had to look at what you did. you knew. my father was dead. I wanted my mother. I have four kids. I can still hear you telling people. I am one of them. when you cried I made room in my bed. when I told you I was hurt you said you didn’t want to hear.
