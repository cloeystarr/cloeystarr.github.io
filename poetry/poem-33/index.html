Poem 32 · Chloe Starr

i open the love spell bottle the perfume is so enticing i am in love with its intoxication i shouldn’t but i do drop it on my eyelids chaos without intention my headache is gone a heartache awaits curses for getting this high on magic i can feel it working already my heart is beating faster and the scent will not leave my nose action rising.
