Poem 37 · Chloe Starr

Mirror parallel universes when i stare into the silver haze not only my current face comes to meet my gape. occasionally, i see crying. i see the tears caused by love affair washed down the sink. i see smiles and healing. i see speeches i've yet to give. i see myself getting older. i see it, all being ok.
