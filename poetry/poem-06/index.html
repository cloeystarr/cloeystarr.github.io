Poem 6 · Chloe Starr

I am filled with light. Praise to the universe. Bring me to my home, fill my pockets enough to pay for it and my dreams of making films. Continue to give me strength in darkness that it will all pour to light for me. Love and light and the best are invited to enter me. Swell my heart.
