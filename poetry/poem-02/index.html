Poem 2 · Chloe Starr

Being present A hard construct, filters include most activities besides being still. Still is the nature of nature Kale is creeping out of my skin force fed myself pounds of the green stuff Enjoy the present moment it is a gift clouds of doubt and fear isolate your bright sun with fog
