# Portals writing

Chloe's original writing is the first reference for her voice. Read it in context
through the private `cloeystarr/chloe-writing-library` repository and the local
archive-recall / chloe-voice skills. Do not put private originals, names, archive
exports or working receipts into this public repository.

Write a piece someone would enjoy reading. Begin with what is interesting in this
particular story: an encounter, image, argument, joke, problem or curiosity.
Let the structure and rhythm follow it. Preserve intelligence, ambiguity and room
for an idea to move. Do not impose a lesson, takeaway, framework, numbered list,
professional-development persona, audience question or tidy moral. a16z is a
reference for editorial energy and specificity; Chloe's writing supplies the voice.
Do not manufacture a punchline at the end of every paragraph either.

Use the actual archive and verified sources for nonfiction. Do not invent feelings,
conversations, attendance, results or finished work. Keep finished poems, direct
quotes and recorded speech intact. Credit other people's work and distinguish
observed events from imagined examples and AI interpretations. Keep necessary
production detail in captions or source notes when it interrupts the story.
No em dashes or profanity in new public copy written as Chloe.

The current 41 journal pages are authored editions. Their `index.html` files hold
the approved layout and copy. Corresponding records in `script.js`,
`history-posts.js` and `content/editorial-additions.json` carry matching prose and
decks for alternate views. They have `presentation: "authored"` and an
`authored_path`; `scripts/build_portals.py` must not replace them with an older
selection of source sections. Update both the page and its content record when
editing. Check the article description, sharing metadata and Portals card too.
Retain the media treatments, source links, film players, credits and dated status.

Read every revised article as an article, including its ending. Check the full
collection for repeated rhetorical habits. A machine check cannot certify voice.
Run the editorial media check and JavaScript syntax checks before release. Keep
private review records outside the deploy tree. Existing-article repair and new
publication are separate scopes; new drafts retain their review requirement.
