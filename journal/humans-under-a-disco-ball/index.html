Humans under a disco ball.

At SHACK15, the week’s building conversations gave way to music, people and a room I wanted to remember.

Words & work by Chloe Starr · Retrospective written October 21, 2026

Agent harnesses by day. Humans under a disco ball by night. That was the way I introduced the SHACK15 film when I put it out.

Chris and I are in the photographs together, lit purple by the room. The film moves through the gathering and the music rather than trying to turn the night into a technical recap. It belongs in the same collection as the talks because the people and places are part of the week too.

Another person’s camera can widen the night

The finished edit also includes additional DJ and Bay Bridge footage from Rick Uzcategui. That contribution is credited in the original thread. It should stay visible here too. A montage can put several perspectives in one sequence without pretending one person filmed all of it.

The music is District Four by Kevin MacLeod, excerpted and mixed under CC BY 4.0. That is the soundtrack credit, separate from the DJ footage. Identifying both makes it possible to follow the sources behind the feeling of the piece.

Further notes, people & sources

A room is more than its location

SHACK15 brings entrepreneurs and creative communities together in the Ferry Building. Its program includes speakers, music and cultural events. That gives a clearer sense of the setting than using a venue name as a label beneath a clip.

The pictures in this article are our own record of being there. One holds us together in the room. Another holds the light and the disco ball. They give the film a physical place to return to when the week’s conversations have started running together.

Let the small films keep their own register

The point of this cut is the shift from building conversations to people together at night. It does not need a new claim about agents to justify its place. In the larger collection, a film like this keeps the city and the company of the week in view beside the more researched pieces.
