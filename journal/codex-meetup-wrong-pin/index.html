Wrong pin, right people.

Getting to the monthly Codex meetup became the opening of a film about meeting builders in person.

Words & work by Chloe Starr · Retrospective written October 21, 2026

The original caption starts with the wrong pin. Then we found the monthly Codex meetup together. That small interruption gives the gathering a beginning instead of opening with a list of sponsors.

From the gathering to the cut

The post thanks OpenAI, SCN Foundry, UpScaleX and Techweek. What I singled out was seeing people get started with Codex in person. That is the scale of the piece: one gathering, and the people beginning to use a tool I spend so much time with.

The film uses Neon by Scott Buckley, excerpted and mixed under CC BY 4.0. Its later YouTube placement preserves the original X post and music source. The website now gives that same film a place beside the rest of the work.

An arrival can carry the story

The clip opens with us finding our way together. Then the room comes into view: a meetup, people learning, and a place to begin using Codex in person. It is a small film, and it benefits from staying close to that small event.

The photograph at the top comes from the original arrival footage. It keeps me in the story, rather than opening with a screenshot of a tool. The room picture shows where the arrival leads. Together they give the short cut a beginning and a destination.

The published caption thanks OpenAI, SCN Foundry, UpScaleX and Techweek. That credit belongs here as well. The film’s subject is people getting started together, in a room those organizations made possible. OpenAI’s Codex page gives a reader a route into the tool itself, separate from my record of the meetup.

The original thread, YouTube copy and this article preserve the same work. They are different entry points into one experience, with the soundtrack attribution kept intact.
