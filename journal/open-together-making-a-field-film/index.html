Field film / Retrospective / October 16, 2026

Open source, out on the dance floor.

Giant hugs, little robots and a dance floor became a field film about open source and owning our creative work.

Words & work by Chloe Starr · Retrospective written October 21, 2026

The Midway gave this film several kinds of movement: demonstrations, people playing, and the dance floor. The published description keeps those pieces together under Open Together.

Open source has people around it

Hugging Face gives the machine-learning community places to publish models, datasets and applications. Its Spaces make some of that work something a visitor can try, rather than only read about. That is a useful connection to a night of demonstrations and conversation.

My film follows the gathering through the playful details as well as the demos. A giant hug and a little robot belong to the same night as a conversation about owning creative work. The point of the edit is to let someone encounter the room, not turn every frame into a product announcement.

Thank you to Hugging Face, hosts Jeff Boudier and Joseph Spisak, and event sponsors Arcee AI and Reflection AI, named in the published credits. The release also thanks the wider open-source community. Its attendee-footage credits name Prasad Pilla, BDT Talent Group and Glenn. Their source links travel with the film so another person’s contribution does not become anonymous texture in my edit.
