Building the future, still buffering.

At Build Personal Agents Hack, the first obstacle was getting connected.

Words & work by Chloe Starr · Retrospective written October 21, 2026

I came to Build Personal Agents Hack to build a personal agent. The first task was convincing the WiFi to work. That is the opening preserved in my original post.

From the gathering to the cut

There is room for that in a film about building. The infrastructure of a day includes the connection, the people helping, and the things that happen before a demonstration is ready. The published clip keeps the scale of that experience rather than claiming a finished agent.

Keep the sources with the film

The event credits name Neon, Mastra, Exa, Fly.io, Kernel, assistant-ui, AgentMail, Executor and CodeRabbit. Exa also supplied the emotional support goat mentioned in the post. The original clip carries a small, recognizable part of the day: trying to begin.

The stack has more than one job

The companies in the event credits are not one interchangeable collection of AI logos. Neon provides a Postgres-based backend for apps and agents. Exa provides web search and retrieval for agents. An agent needs somewhere to keep information and a way to find information; those are distinct parts of the problem.

The original credits also name Mastra, Fly.io, Kernel, assistant-ui, AgentMail, Executor and CodeRabbit. I’m keeping those names attached to the gathering without claiming I used every product or shipped an integration with each of them.

My film is twelve seconds long. Its contribution is an honest little moment from the day: arriving to build something ambitious, then meeting the connection problem in front of us. A short field note can be specific about that without padding the story with a product launch that did not happen.

Thank you to the event crew and fellow builders. The source recording and original caption establish this moment in the room; the company links let a reader continue into the work behind the gathering.
