Field film · November 19, 2026

Two hearts,
to go.

A quick maker afternoon.
A matching pair to take home.

By Chloe Starr · At DD’s workshop, November 14

Chris and I stopped by Didi’s keychain workshop on Saturday and made matching hearts for ourselves. A quick weekend visit. Two little things to take home together.

The event was called 3D Printed NFC Keychains. Our contribution to the afternoon was apparently to make it romantic.

Chris and I stopped by @finding_dd’s 3D-printed keychain workshop and made matching hearts for ourselves. Apparently we can turn a quick weekend outing into couples’ merch. AI paper collage from our real photographs.

Chloe Starr · November 19, 2026

Mint edges, lilac circuit lines, a little heart inside the heart. At the table, my website was open on the phone beside the pieces.

Didi makes the kind of things that are fun to discover someone has actually built. On her Instagram, there’s a photo-booth purse that prints pictures on receipt paper. She programmed the printout for a friend’s birthday. A camera, a printer, a bag, and a very particular person to make it for.

Put that beside our matching hearts and there’s a small idea I want to keep: make something for someone. Give the technology an occasion.

Didi built a photo-booth purse that prints pictures on receipt paper, customized for a friend’s birthday. A bag, a camera, a printer, a person to make it for. Give the technology an occasion.

Chloe Starr · November 19, 2026

We only stayed briefly. We still came away with a pair.
