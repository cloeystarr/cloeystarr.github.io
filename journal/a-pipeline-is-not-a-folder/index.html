Process / Retrospective / October 13, 2026

A pipeline is more than a folder of exports.

The export succeeded. Unfortunately, I still had to watch it.

Words & work by Chloe Starr · Retrospective written October 21, 2026

The file exported. It played. It still did not show the thing I had asked for.

In the October 13 audit, this kept happening. An image illustrated a word in the narration while ignoring the action. A transition worked technically and looked terrible. A face held together until it moved. The folder was full of successful exports.

What changed on screen?

The audit used scripts, production records, continuity notes and my feedback. It was not a fresh full viewing of every film. Even the account of what we had checked needed to stop claiming more than had happened.

If someone passes an object, I need to see the object get to the other person. A nearby flame, however beautiful, will not perform the handoff. We started describing the before, the action and the after, including who needed to remain recognizable through it.

The sound needed its own attention

The music could stop abruptly while the pictures carried on congratulating themselves. I needed voices, room tone, effects and score to stay separately editable. Fixing the face would not fix the sound.

I had not proved we were faster. I had become better at describing why I was unhappy with the film. That was a start. The next export would still have to survive being watched.
