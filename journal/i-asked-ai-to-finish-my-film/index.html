Production diary / October 21, 2026

I Asked AI to Finish My Film. We’re Still Building the Way to Do It.

The face kept changing. The clips kept arriving. The movie still wasn’t finished.

Words & work by Chloe Starr

The teeth weren’t mine.

That became one of the problems with turning Where Do Memories Live? into a film. The images could look cinematic and still change my eyes, my jaw, or my mouth. Across different scenes, the cinematic version of me kept becoming a different person.

I supplied original photographs and videos because this film comes from my life. Those materials were references for creating something new. I wanted a film with a cast, environments, outfits, performances, camera angles, and emotional reactions. The preparation we would need if we were about to shoot with actors.

Instead, we accumulated storyboard packages, repair attempts, research, and little clips. Each came with another explanation of what remained unresolved.

Eventually, I asked for the entire recut using Wan on Vast.ai, with a total budget of $10. The latest attempt spent $5.84 and left a six-second silent candidate. It did not deliver the movie.

I stopped the generations.

The film has to remain mine

I’m the protagonist of my autobiographical art. My face and body belong to the storytelling, just as my writing and memories do. I’ve used Frida Kahlo as an analogy for that practice.

My archive also contains years of collected taste. The films, images, artists, music, and books I’ve saved should inform actual choices. What does the room look like? Why does the camera move? What happens to the person inside the image?

A beautiful image doesn’t answer those questions by itself.

For the Coffee poem, I eventually identified roughly the first 25 seconds as the movie. The later footage could be saved, but the poem had finished. Continuing the montage took us away from what we were making.

That correction belongs in the method. Generating more material creates editorial choices. It doesn’t create an obligation to use everything.

Further notes, people & sources

What a finished workflow has to connect

I’ve supplied examples because I can see the possibilities. The next step is to study how those artists construct their films and carry useful methods into production.

One useful example is Every Scenario, directed by Sara Nemati. Its published breakdown connects character references, sound timing, animation, and separately editable graphics. It also records rejected treatments and finishing versions. There is a director making judgments throughout.

Its 6,652-credit figure covers the generated clips used in the final cut. Style frames and song generations are excluded. That is not a complete production budget, or a price for my film.

What interests me is the connection between the decisions. Sound timing reaches the edit. Character preparation reaches the shots. A correction can change one layer without throwing away everything that worked.

What we carry forward

We’re keeping a running list of approaches, sources, and conditions for calling something successful. The priorities are practical:

  • Keep identity, wardrobe, locations, and props distinct. Rejected generated faces cannot become references for who I am.
  • Direct an action, its reaction, and the changed scene together. Narration cannot supply a performance that never appears.
  • Use the existing free Blender work for cameras and blocking. Proxy movement does not establish final likeness.
  • Design sound alongside picture, keeping voice, music, ambience, and effects separately editable.
  • Repair the defective shot or layer while preserving successful material.
  • Price the whole film, including setup, downloads, idle rental time, rejected takes, audio, and finishing.

This is a research and implementation list. It is not permission to restart paid experiments, or to turn the deliverable into another isolated clip.

We still lack dependable identity preservation across the performances this film requires. We also lack a reliable whole-film price and a completed audiovisual result at my quality bar.

The next production has to demonstrate improvement in the film itself. The same person across the cuts. An action that reaches its consequence. A soundtrack that belongs to the poem.

I still want to give this system a poem and receive a finished movie.
