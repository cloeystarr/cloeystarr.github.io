Field film / Retrospective / October 3, 2026

Before the week, waffles.

A small gathering with Chris became one of the early films in the collection.

Words & work by Chloe Starr · Retrospective written October 21, 2026

Waffles, good company, and a very hot day with Chris. That is how the original description places this little film, as San Francisco locals prepared for hospitality week and people coming to visit HQ.

A little film does not need to become a keynote

The photograph of Chris and me gives this piece its scale. We are out together as the city gets ready for visitors. The sun, the company and the gathering are enough material for a small film.

The cut uses brief written observations and music rather than an elaborate explanatory narration. That decision keeps the piece close to the afternoon. It also makes the difference between this field note and the later panel films easy to see: the collection is growing, but every piece does not have to speak in the same register.

Chris Starr AI is credited as part of the gathering and the published film. The music source and license remain linked in the release notes. The original post keeps the gathering personal: waffles, company and a hot day.

The larger production story is that these small recordings are becoming findable together. This article, the film and its original post give the afternoon a place in the collection without pretending it was a different kind of event.

From the gathering to the cut

A collection of field films can hold these smaller gatherings alongside panels and demonstrations. This one does not need to become a lesson about technology to belong. It records another part of the week and the people I was spending it with.
