Field film / Sound / October 20, 2026

A night out comes back to the edit.

Overmind at Joey the Cat, a waveform-editing conversation, and the way the films I’m making keep finding their way into each other.

Words & work by Chloe Starr

Chris and I went out to play. I came back with another field film, and a conversation that made it into the work.

Overmind brought the neighborhood together at Joey the Cat. The film moves through small models, a skee-ball champion, and a bad pitch that wandered into my poetry film. That last connection is why this belongs in the production journal as well as the Events gallery.

A conversation becomes a tool

I saw Alex Newman again. His waveform-editing tip made it into this film. The conversation became material for the edit, alongside the footage and my own voice. It’s one of the reasons I want to keep making these field notes: what happens at the gathering can continue in the work afterward.

The cut also responds to Overmind’s public artwork and its identity by New Genre Studio. Those are visual references, credited as such. The film is my account of the night, made with Chris.

The sound has its own sources

The music is Upbeat Overworld by Wolfgang_, released as CC0. The original voice and footage sit with that cue in the edit. Keeping track of those pieces matters when the same film travels from X to YouTube and onto this site. The credits need to travel too.

The work behind the room

The Overmind identity by New Genre Studio is a source of visual inspiration named in the film credits. The arcade footage is our own. Keeping both visible makes it possible to follow what came from the event and what came from another studio’s design work.

Thank you to Overmind and Joey the Cat for the gathering, and Alex Newman for the waveform-editing conversation. Alex’s Claude-Mem project is a separate path into his work on persistent context. Chris Starr AI is credited with me in the film. The original thread carries the complete acknowledgments.
