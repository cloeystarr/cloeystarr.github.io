Field film / Sound / October 20, 2026

A night out comes back to the edit.

Skee-ball, small models, a bad pitch, and an editing tip that followed me home from Joey the Cat.

Words & work by Chloe Starr

Chris and I went out to play. As usual, the films came with us.

Overmind had brought people to Joey the Cat. There were small models, a skee-ball champion and a bad pitch that somehow found a second life in my poetry film. My work has a habit of meeting itself on a night out.

The waveform comes home

I saw Alex Newman again. His tip about editing along the waveform made it into this cut. A conversation, then a different way of hearing where an image should end. I like that I can go out for one thing and come home with a new way into another.

The cut also responds to Overmind’s public artwork and its identity by New Genre Studio. Those are visual references, credited as such. The film is my account of the night, made with Chris.

The Overmind identity by New Genre Studio is a source of visual inspiration named in the film credits. The arcade footage is our own. Keeping both visible makes it possible to follow what came from the event and what came from another studio’s design work.
